01 / about

About me

I am an Assistant Professor of Industrial Engineering at An-Najah National University and Coordinator of its Master of Engineering Management program. I lead curriculum development and academic planning, and I serve as Associate Editor of the An-Najah University Journal for Research B (Natural Sciences).

Beside academia, I train professionals in data science and analytics. At Udacity I trained 800+ professionals across the MENA region with a 94% satisfaction score. I published a course on DataCamp and taught about 200 students at Correlation One. My goal: bridge engineering and data-driven decision making.
